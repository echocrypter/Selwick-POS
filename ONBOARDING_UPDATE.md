# First-time business onboarding

- New installations: Sign Up -> Username + 4-digit PIN -> Business Name, Address, Phone and Business Type -> Create Owner Account.
- Industry categories are saved automatically. Other / Custom allows comma-separated categories.
- The business profile is saved in the existing `pos_settings` format, and categories/type in `pos_categories` and `selwick_offline_business_type`.
- Sign In and later cashier signups do not ask for business setup again.
- Existing business data and local accounts are not reset. Settings -> Change Business Type remains available.
- Data is stored in this browser's local storage, not across different devices unless restored from backup.
- The application build has not been run in this environment.
